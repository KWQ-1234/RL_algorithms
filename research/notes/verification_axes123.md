## WRONG / NOT FOUND / needs rewording

No claim is outright WRONG. The items below need fixes or qualifiers.

- **#4 VC-PPO**: label 5.6 / 29.4 / 41.9 as **8k-context ablations** (the 16k result is 48.8 vs GRPO 38.9). The paper says the default λ_actor is 0.95, yet Table 2's VC-PPO row equals the λ = 0.99 row. Cite Table 4 directly for the λ sweep.
- **#7f GenAC tuning**: per-method tuning is NOT FOUND. All methods share one verl-default hyperparameter table, and RLOO has a single run.
- **#8b RAFT++**: the "discard all-wrong prompts" evidence is LLaMA-3.2-3B training-reward curves (Fig. 4), not the Qwen-7B table. The conclusion says "entirely correct and incorrect".
- **#9b Yue et al.**: the "> 40 points" gap holds only on the in-domain Omni-MATH test split. On the train split RLOO, R++ and DAPO are below 40, and on MATH500 the gap is about 21. The text's 43.9/42.6 do not match Table 3.
- **#13 Dr. GRPO**: "statistically significant" has no test behind it (3-run mean ± std). The 3-seed comparison covers only the joint removal; the separate ablations in Fig. 8 have an unstated seed count.
- **#14a Tricks or Traps**: the std-removal ablation uses a batch-level mean.
- **#15b MaxRL**: reword "≈ 1/p". MaxRL(T) weight is (1−(1−p)^T)/p, which tends to the ML weight 1/p only as T → ∞, in the low-pass-rate regime.
- **#17a 2601.23135**: "indistinguishable at 7B" holds only for the largely-solved MATH L1–5 run. On the harder L3–5 run per-prompt std pulls ahead.
- **#20c LOOP**: "about 9 points" is from Table 1 (best checkpoint, 71.3 → 61.9). With the 3-run 66.4 ± 4.8 (Table 2) the drop is 6.8 pp.
- **#23 GLM-4.5**: model size NOT FOUND (only "smaller experimental model"). The evidence is figure-only, and the gain is in convergence speed (final 46.5% vs 46.3%).
- **#24 MiniMax-M1**: the exact aggregation formula is NOT FOUND. Quote "combined sample-level loss and token-level normalization" rather than paraphrasing it as a switch.
- **#26a verl**: "global batch" = the PPO mini-batch summed over DP ranks (one optimizer step), not the whole rollout batch.
- **Figure-only evidence** (no numbers in text): #1, #2, #3a, #7d, #11, #12a, #14a/b, #22, #23.
